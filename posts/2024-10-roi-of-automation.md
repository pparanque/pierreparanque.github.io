# The ROI of Automation in Finance

Automation is no longer a luxury — it’s the backbone of modern finance operations.
By reducing repetitive manual tasks, finance teams can shift focus to insight and strategy.

### Key Benefits
- Shorter closing cycles
- Fewer manual errors
- Stronger business partnering
- Better visibility on performance

> “Automation doesn’t replace finance — it *empowers* it.”

*Published October 2024 – Pierre Paranque*
